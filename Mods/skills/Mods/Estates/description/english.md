Improves **Estates** into a stronger economic support skill.

Estates preserves the daily gold income from Horn of the Abyss for the hero's owner and additionally improves Marketplace exchange rates for the entire team.

Gold income from multiple Estates heroes stacks for their respective owners, while their Marketplace bonuses support the whole team.

## Skill Effects

* **Basic Estates:** +250 gold per day for the owning player; team exchange rates as if it had 1 additional Marketplace
* **Advanced Estates:** +500 gold per day for the owning player; team exchange rates as if it had 2 additional Marketplaces
* **Expert Estates:** +1000 gold per day for the owning player; team exchange rates as if it had 3 additional Marketplaces

## Specialist Interaction

Lord Haart's Estates specialty increases the gold income generated for his owner, but does not further improve Marketplace exchange rates.

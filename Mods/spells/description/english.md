A collection of spell reworks and behavior improvements designed for use with Horn of the Abyss on VCMI.

These modules aim to make underused or overly narrow spells more practical, more attractive, and more meaningful to cast, while preserving their original tactical identity whenever possible.

## Included Spell Changes

* **Mirth** — increases Morale and sets a minimum positive Morale result for affected allied units while the spell lasts. Morale-lowering artifacts and creature effects, such as morale reduction from **Bone Dragons** and **Ghost Dragons**, cannot reduce affected units below that minimum. The artifact **Spirit of Oppression** can still suppress positive Morale entirely.

* **Sorrow** — decreases Morale and sets a maximum negative Morale result for affected enemy units while the spell lasts. Morale-raising artifacts and creature effects, such as morale bonuses from **Angels** and **Archangels**, cannot raise affected units above that cap.

* **Slayer** — becomes a more practical anti-elite combat spell. Without Fire Magic, it keeps its original behavior. **Basic Fire Magic** expands it to all level 7 and higher creatures, **Advanced Fire Magic** allows repeated casts to stack on the same allied stack, and **Expert Fire Magic** turns it into a mass spell.

* **Magic Mirror** — becomes a more flexible defensive spell. Higher levels of **Air Magic** add a chance to resist hostile spells, while **Expert Air Magic** turns it into a mass spell. **Magic Mirror** can work together with **New Luck** modules, allowing protected stacks to receive controlled bonuses to spell reflection chance when the relevant modules are enabled.

* **Acid Burst** — adds a new level 4 Water Magic debuff spell designed for prolonged battles. Higher mastery levels strengthen its effects, ultimately affecting all enemy stacks.

The **Slayer** module also includes a HotA-like correction for **Coronius**, updating his Slayer specialty and description.

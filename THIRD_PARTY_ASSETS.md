# Third-Party Assets

Hirki Plus Patch contains modified, reused, or reconstructed material based on assets and visual designs from existing games and projects.

This file documents their general provenance. Hirki Plus Patch does not claim ownership of the underlying third-party material.

## Might and Magic VI

Modified Might and Magic VI artwork is used in:

- **Armageddon's Blade** — `Armag. Blade` spell artwork
- **Boots of Reinforcement** — artifact artwork
- **Leather Armor of Swiftness** — artifact artwork
- **Acid Burst** — spell icons

## VCMI Reinforcements mod

- **Reinforcements** — spell artwork from the VCMI `Reinforcements` mod by George King, with graphics by Ben Yan. The upstream mod is distributed under CC BY-SA 4.0.

## Heroes of Might and Magic III

Original or modified Heroes III assets are used in:

- **Acid Burst** — recolored Rust Dragon animation
- **Zombies / Grave Ripple** — Zombie assets and modified Death Ripple animation and icons
- **Hydras** — Hydra / Chaos Hydra animations and selected spell/effect assets
- **Bloodwake III** — artwork based on the Frenzy spell
- **Priests** — unused original Monk spellcasting animation
- **Cerberus** — additional animation built from original Cerberus frames
- **Melodia** — original Heroes III graphics included locally for the hero override
- **Roland** — original Heroes III graphics included locally for the hero override
- **Ammo Cart** — original Heroes III assets used for the recreated Ammo Cart
- **New Intelligence** — Advanced Intelligence artwork

Some HPP units and modules reference existing Heroes III, VCMI, or Horn of the Abyss resources directly from the installed game/mod environment. These runtime references are not redistributed HPP assets unless a local copy is included in the mod.

This also applies to dependencies and references such as `hota`, `hota.cove`, `hota.factory`, and `hota.bulwark`.

## Doom Eternal

- **Cerberus / Threefold Bite** — short sound sample

## Planescape: Torment

- **Ice Storm** — visual reconstruction of the `Stygian Ice Storm` spell from Planescape: Torment. The HPP artwork and fullscreen animation were recreated for HPP based on the original visual design and animation sequence rather than by directly reusing the original Planescape: Torment animation frames or icon assets.
